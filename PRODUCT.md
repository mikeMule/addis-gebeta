# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

(Ionic React + Capacitor, shipped as a mobile app on iOS and Android; one web design language, iOS-mode Ionic components.)

## Users

People in Addis Ababa ordering food to their home or office from a phone, usually hungry, often one-handed, deciding quickly between nearby restaurants. Secondary job: sending a parcel or picking up groceries/other items across town.

## Product Purpose

Addis Gebeta is a food-first delivery app: browse restaurants and dishes (local Habesha food alongside burgers, pizza, shawarma, chicken), build a cart, check out, and follow the order live until it arrives. Success means a user goes from opening the app to a placed order in under a minute and always knows where the order is.

## Positioning

A local, Addis-native table: Ethiopian dishes and neighbourhood restaurants sit first-class next to international fast food, with areas (Bole, Kirkos, Kazanchis, Piassa…) and ETB pricing as native vocabulary. "Gebeta" is the shared table.

## Operating Context

- Delivery addresses are Addis neighbourhoods; prices in ETB; payment is cash on delivery (mock).
- Multi-service top row: Food (primary), Parcel (existing send flow), Groceries, Books, Gifts, Pets — non-food services beyond Parcel are placeholders.
- Live order tracking with courier, ETA and timeline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend yet: restaurants, menus, offers and order progress are mock data / simulated in the client.
- Food imagery loads from Unsplash URLs at runtime (requires internet).
- Restaurants and brands are fictional with original monogram logos; never use real brand names or logos.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Addis Gebeta** (replaces the earlier working name "Bahiran").
- User supplied a visual reference (a red/coral food delivery home screen: service tiles, "Deliver to" bar, photo category row, offer tickets, brand grid, filter chips, restaurant list) as the binding inspiration for the redesign.
- Needs a polished splash screen.

## Evidence on Hand

No real restaurants, reviews, ratings, offers, or partners exist. All of these are synthetic demo content and must stay labelled/understood as mock data; do not present them as real partnerships.

## Product Principles

1. Food first, order fast: every screen shortens the path to a placed order.
2. Local is first-class: Ethiopian food and Addis places are default, not exotic.
3. Always know the state: cart, price, ETA and order status are visible and honest.
4. Sharp and precise: aligned icons and text, consistent rhythm; polish is the brand.
